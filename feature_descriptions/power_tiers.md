# Power tiers

Power cards show Basic (green), Rare (purple), or Legendary (warm gold) borders and an explicit tier label. Selection and active-power cards share the same presentation. Animal-only / all-matches scope labels remain separate from tier borders.

Each power has an editable `tier` in the shared content catalog. The designer supports native and custom tier changes, and level copies retain their tier. Missing tier metadata defaults to Basic for older content. Clones and goal changes retain the authored tier.

Initial assignments: simple fixed rewards are Basic; animal-specific growth, Bloom transformations, and utility powers are Rare; global growth and premium engines are Legendary. These are starting balance classifications. Tier does not change power behavior, eligibility, or draft probability.
