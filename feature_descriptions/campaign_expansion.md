# Campaign expansion: levels 12–51

40 additional five-round levels. Themed choices appear in rounds 2 and 4; round 1 supplies a starter and rounds 3 and 5 use the ordinary compatible pool. Every campaign level now has a final boss; see [the boss roster](campaign_bosses.md). Powers continue to accumulate.

Roster rounds contain only add/remove animal powers; compatibility determines the possible additions/removals. Points/budget and clears/budget rounds supply two progress cards and one budget card. Budget powers earn time or moves through their stated match patterns, rather than an immediate grant. Shuffle rounds supply a shuffle option alongside two progress cards. Pattern rounds offer three compatible tactical scoring powers. Owned non-repeatable powers and incompatible animals/rules remain excluded. Exhausted pools offer fewer cards rather than unrelated replacements.

Targets and budgets are initial tuning, not verified difficulty ratings. Growth is gentler for block goals and roster tradeoffs.

Budget upgrades in budget-themed levels, and shuffle upgrades in shuffle-themed levels, are reserved for themed rounds so ordinary offers cannot exhaust those choices early. The existing campaign progression unlocks these levels after level 11.

## Brainstorm: future mechanics (not implemented)

- **Pick a habitat:** three roster bundles, each adding one animal and removing another. Preview the board and multiplier.
- **Cash out or carry on:** take immediate points, add seconds, or bank a multiplier for the next round.
- **Changing seasons:** refill direction rotates each round.
- **Rescue mission:** clear a named animal quota, with protection against removing the target animal.
- **Tiny zoo:** start with fewer animals and lower scoring; introduce variety for bigger rewards.
- **Power pairs:** choose one of three two-power bundles with complementary triggers.
- **Borrowed time:** gain time now in exchange for a higher target next round.
- **No-shuffle challenge:** exchange paid shuffles for extra moves while retaining dead-board recovery.

Playtest roster removals, line-mode budget triggers, and limited-move block goals first. Track success by round and selected power before raising targets.
