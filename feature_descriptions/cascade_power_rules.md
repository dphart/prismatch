# Cascades and power triggers

Falling matches are resolved match groups. Extra physical tiles cleared by a power are not match groups and do not retrigger powers. A power can reject cascade progress while still applying its previously earned reward to a cascade; these are separate behaviors.

| Power family | Can cascades trigger or advance it? | Can cascades receive its reward? |
|---|---|---|
| Animal Mastery (Points/Multiplier) | No | No; both progression and earned bonuses are player-match-only |
| Animal Rivalry | Yes, including rival penalties | Yes, for the target animal |
| Animal Growth | Yes; counts matched animal tiles | Yes; animal-specific in Blocks, global in Points |
| Fixed animal bonuses / Animal Double | Yes, when the animal matches | Yes |
| Exact-size, minimum-size, maximum-size bonuses | Yes, when their conditions match | Yes |
| Consistency Is Key | Yes; cascades can extend or reset the size streak | Yes |
| Collector / shape-credit powers | Yes, when the objective and shape qualify | Yes |
| Sweep powers | Yes, when their condition qualifies | Yes; Chain Reaction specifically requires a cascade |
| Habitat Bounty / Amplifier | Yes, when the condition qualifies | Yes |
| Rapids Engine | Yes; requires cascades | Yes |
| Other habitat Engines | No growth from cascades | Yes; already earned growth applies |
| Tactical starting powers / growing foundations | No growth from cascades | Yes; baseline and already earned bonuses apply |
| Edge Balance | Yes; edge/interior changes affect later matches | Yes |
| Bloom transformations | No | No transformations from cascades |
| Budget powers | No | No time/move payouts from cascades |
| Shuffle Streak / Square Ticket / Big Ticket | No ticket progress | Stored free shuffles remain usable |
| Color Focus | No; only player matches update its animal streak | No doubling on cascades |
| Twin Shot / Four Fury / Jackpot Five / Precision Strike | Only player matches arm the next-match reward | In Points, the next resolved match can be a cascade and consume the reward |
| Shuffle Spark | Player shuffle arms the next successful play | Yes; the whole rewarded play includes cascades |
| Magnet / roster modifiers / matching restrictions | Passive; no match-trigger progress | Their ongoing rules remain active during cascades |
| Structured/custom powers | According to trigger: cascade-only, player-initial-only, or any qualifying group | According to authored reward and trigger |

Mastery retains its earned bonus across rounds, but cascades neither change its progress nor receive that bonus. They also do not reset its progress. Rivalry remains a distinct rule and retains cascade behavior.
