# Campaign opening variety

Only levels 4 (Size Matters) and 5 (Clear Commitments) offer the 3/4/5 match foundations. Later block levels match 4+ by default and offer three themed powers:

| Level | Opening choices | Starting target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 12 ? Cascade Cleanup | Chain Reaction, Helping Hand, Edge Explorer | 32 |
| 13 ? Big Group Grove | Trail Blazer, Large Dividend, Big Breather | 32 |
| 14 ? Square Deal | Garden Excavator, Square Dividend, Square Deal | 34 |
| 15 ? Westward Sweep | Shore Sweep, Horizontal Dividend, Edge Explorer | 30 |
| 16 ? Rising Rescue | Chain Reaction, Vertical Dividend, Helping Hand | 36 |
| 17 ? Last Move Market | Big Breather, Square Deal, Edge Explorer | 36 |
| 20 ? Shuffle School | Helping Hand, Big Sweep, Edge Explorer | 32 |
| 21 ? Square Shuffle | Square Deal, Garden Excavator, Square Dividend | 36 |
| 22 ? Big Remix | Big Breather, Trail Blazer, Large Dividend | 34 |

Each matched tile now gives one block credit before other bonuses. Starting targets were halved to compensate for removing Steady Clears (two credits per matched tile). Growth, budgets, level bonuses, bosses and later offers remain as authored. Existing device-specific scaling overrides take precedence until reset. Shuffle tools and payoff choices remain in rounds 2 and 3, avoiding early ownership shortages.

Shared content is permanent in `Assets/Resources/Content/prismatch.json`; restart Unity Play or rebuild to load it. The in-game scaling export/import continues to patch only targets and growth, preserving these opening pools.
