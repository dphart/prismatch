# Wild Release (level 31)

Points goal, 7x7 connected board, four-tile default matching, in-place refills and six rounds. Initial tuning: 1,000 points, 30% target growth, 36 moves, and 4 level base points per tile. These are provisional balance values. The level uses shared animal, base, multiplier, budget and shuffle powers; it creates no per-level power copies. Existing levels and powers are unchanged.

One empty cage reserves a board space at all times, starting at the center. The cage cannot be swapped, matched, or cleared by sweeping powers. Each resolved matching group that touches an orthogonal neighbor adds one charge, regardless of how many of its tiles touch. Cascades count. Diagonal-only contact does not count. At 10/10 the door opens, the meter pulses gold, and the status invites the player to feed an animal. The cage stays ready while the player makes other matches or shuffles.

Drag any visible animal from anywhere on the board into the open cage. This gesture works even when ordinary swipe-to-move is disabled. Tapping an animal and then the cage does the same thing. With swipe-to-move enabled, dragging to another ordinary tile still performs an ordinary swap. Closed cages, invalid drops, paused input and overlapping resolutions reject feeds without spending a move.

A feed costs one move and resolves every visible tile of the chosen animal as **one player-created matching group**, using a snapshot before refill. Disconnected tiles join that group. All normal base, multiplier, animal, shape, growth, queued and budget hooks run once for the group, with its full tile count and actual positions. There is no hidden extra multiplier. The empty cage placeholder is excluded from both score and animal progress. The feeding animal animates into the cage; all chosen animals pop and clear together. Refills and resulting cascades resolve normally, without extra move costs. The final move finishes resolving before the outcome is checked.

After the clearing wave, the cage moves to a different space and starts at 0/10. It prefers surviving tiles outside the cleared area; a whole-board release can use a space that will be refilled. The old empty cage space receives a fresh animal without scoring it. New cascades can charge the new cage. Cage position and charge survive shuffles; a new round, retry or run resets the cage to the center at 0/10. Cancelling a feed before its clear restores the same ready cage.

The tile shows a cage frame, animated opening door, progress meter and counter. The briefing animates nearby matches, opening, dragging a Duck in, all Ducks clearing, and the cage reappearing elsewhere. The shared editor recognizes the mechanic and offers ordinary reusable power groups.

Editor playtesting: **Prismatch > Preview > Wild Release** starts the level without changing unlocks. After selecting a power, **Prismatch > Preview > Charge Wild Release Cage** opens the cage without advancing scores or powers, for testing the feed gesture. These commands are editor-only.

Verification covers the pure state machine, board protection, whole-board fallback, cancellation, briefing frames, complete six-round shared drafts, and unchanged recent-level mechanics. Play-mode coverage exercises actual tile drag events, point-power scoring, a single move cost, refill, relocation and drag-ghost cleanup. Difficulty and mobile touch readability require playtesting.

A completed capture also removes its animal type from the refill pool for the rest of the round, before the first refill and its cascades. Shuffles and roster rebuilds preserve the exclusion; cancellation before clearing does not remove anything. New rounds and retries restore the normal roster, including existing roster powers. The cage stops charging and accepting feeds once three animal types remain, preserving the existing minimum roster.

The cage has a recessed metal frame, brass latch and rivets, and a separate charge track. Opening and capture receive a one-second presentation beat. Feeding enlarges the animal briefly, moves it into the cage, closes the barred door, highlights matching animals, then clears them. Countdown and elapsed gameplay time are held during the presentation; matching waits for it to finish. The same pause supports lightning, vault openings and crosswind turns without resuming a manually paused timer.
