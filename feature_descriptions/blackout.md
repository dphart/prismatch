# Blackout — level 32

Every 5 player matches, unprotected squares go black. Clearing darkness colors a square green and protects it for the rest of the round. Matches reveal replacement squares and adjacent neighbors; cascades reveal squares too. Boss: only replacement squares are revealed.

Protection belongs to board positions and stays through swaps and refills. Initially visible squares are not protected until darkness has actually been cleared from them. Each round and retry resets darkness, protection, and the match counter.
