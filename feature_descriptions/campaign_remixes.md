# Campaign remixes: levels 12–19

The live campaign is authored in `Assets/Resources/Content/prismatch.json`. These eight additions follow Changing Tides without changing the first eleven levels or their saved progression IDs. All have six rounds and a final boss. Six additions use block-count goals. The subsequent [shuffle expansion](shuffle_levels.md) adds levels 20–23 and brings the campaign to ten block levels out of 23 total.

Every matched animal counts in these block levels. Their opening offers use the existing 3+/4+/5+ block foundations, followed by clearing, budget and shuffle powers compatible with the goal and mode. Bonus percentages apply to block credit after foundation powers and before boss penalties, using existing whole-number rounding. Points-only garden, spotlight and treasure rewards are reserved for points levels.

Pre-level briefings retain the block-foundation lesson and explain each remix's extra rule and refill direction. Final boss briefings show the boss effect. Targets and budgets are initial tuning; completion rates and phone layouts still need playtesting.

Automated coverage exercises all eight levels across thirty seeded builds each, picking powers through all six rounds, checking full distinct compatible offers, foundation choices, cloning and final bosses. This checks configuration and compatibility, not difficulty or completion rates.
