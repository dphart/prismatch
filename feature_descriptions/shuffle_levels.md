# Shuffle campaign: levels 20–23

These six-round levels follow the first nineteen campaign entries. Existing level IDs and progression remain unchanged. The live campaign now has 23 levels, including 10 block-count levels.

| Level | Name | Goal | Level bonus | Refill | Budget | Starting target | Growth | Final boss |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 20 | Shuffle School | Blocks | None | Down | 85 seconds | 64 | 20% | Wallflower Wolf |
| 21 | Square Shuffle | Blocks | Squares: +25% credit | In place | 90 seconds | 72 | 22% | Box Troll |
| 22 | Big Remix | Blocks | Groups of 6+: +50% credit | Left | 26 moves | 68 | 20% | Stretch Giraffe |
| 23 | Storm Shuffle | Points | Lightning | Down | 85 seconds | 450 | 55% | Cascade Surfer |

Round 1 provides the existing goal-compatible foundation choices. Round 2 offers Fresh Start, Half-Price Shuffle and Shuffle Streak. Round 3 offers Square Ticket, Big Ticket and the goal-specific payoff: Clean Slate for blocks or Shuffle Spark for points. Rounds 4–6 offer one compatible progress power, one progress-or-roster power (including animal magnets), and one shuffle power. Powers accumulate normally and only one power is selected per round.

Pattern-ticket conditions complement the square and large-group level bonuses. Shuffle Spark complements lightning and falling matches. Players can invest in free shuffles, cheaper shuffles or stronger post-shuffle plays. Exact power rules are defined only in [Powers and scoring](powers.md#shuffle-powers).

All levels use the shared native catalog and connected matching. Seeded compatibility checks cover forty complete builds per new level, including opening choices, reserved shuffle rounds, later offers, cloning and final bosses. Targets are provisional and need gameplay balance testing; phone layout and live play remain separate checks.
