# Darkness variations

## Level 33: Animal Spotlight

Two animal types are dark; all others are visible. Every 5 player matches, the next two animal types become dark after the play finishes. Hidden animals still match. Cascades do not advance the counter. The dark pair advances two places through the current animal pool and wraps. New tiles follow their animal type.

## Level 34: Rolling Darkness

Every 2 tiles in player-created matches darken 1 square after the play finishes. Odd tiles carry over. Darkness advances through alternating rows and columns, skipping already dark squares. Matches reveal replacement tiles and adjacent neighbors. Cascades reveal tiles without spreading darkness. Hidden state follows animals through swaps and gravity. Each round and retry resets the sequence, odd-tile remainder, and visibility.
